How to use

Three moves. Then it goes back under the couch.

  1. 01

    Hold

    Hold the handles in front of your chest, grips firm, elbows soft.

  2. 02

    Press

    Press the handles together so the central spring bends. Keep the motion controlled.

  3. 03

    Repeat

    Guide the handles back and repeat. Put it under the couch when you are done.
